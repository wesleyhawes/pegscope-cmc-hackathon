# PegScope operator test

Use this short session with two treasury or operations users before the final recording. Do not explain the expected results before they answer. The offline replay is sufficient; no API key or live market call is needed.

## Setup

- Open the dashboard or offline replay at a readable scale.
- Start with the user on the synthetic scenario labeled **Peg drift**.
- Do not collect names or other personal information. Record participant labels as `A` and `B`.
- Read the task, then stay quiet while the participant works.

## Tasks

### 1. Stablecoin mark

Say: “A treasury holds 100,000 USDC. The displayed price is $0.993. Should the next operator investigate? What is the indicative dollar difference, and what evidence supports your answer?”

Record whether the participant:

- identifies the $700 indicative difference;
- distinguishes a watch from a confirmed incident using separate source updates; and
- describes the data as synthetic rather than a live market observation.

### 2. Stale feed

Switch to **Stale feed** and say: “The page has refreshed, but there has been no new source capture. Is there a current peg mark or dollar estimate?”

Record whether the participant withholds the mark and estimate, identifies the stale or unknown evidence, and avoids treating the page refresh as a new observation.

### 3. Handoff

Ask: “What would you tell the next operator, and what was unclear?” Record one point of confusion or “none.”
