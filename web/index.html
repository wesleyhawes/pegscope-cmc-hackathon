    How alerts are classified

    One basis point is $0.0001 per token at a $1 peg. Negative values indicate a discount and positive values a premium.

    Watch begins at an absolute deviation of 25 bp. Incident requires two distinct, fresh quotes at least 50 bp from $1 on the same side. Two distinct quotes within 10 bp of the peg clear a confirmed incident. Freshness and the maximum gap between confirming observations are both 180 seconds.

    Each quote is a CMC aggregate. Check venue prices and issuer redemption status separately when investigating an alert.
